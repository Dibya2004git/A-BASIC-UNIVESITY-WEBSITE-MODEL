About Maharaj Sriram Chandra Bhanja Deo University

Maharaja Sriram Chandra Bhanja Deo University (MSCBU), formerly known as North Orissa University (NOU), is a public state university established by the Government of Odisha on its foundation date of July 13, 1998. It became fully operational in 1999 after being carved out of Utkal University. On December 23, 2020, it was officially given its new name to honour the visionary ruler of the princely state of Mayurbhanj. Located in Takatpur, Baripada, the main campus is spread across a spacious area of 98.84 acres. The university hosts a diverse array of over 20 academic departments, including Anthropology & Tribal Studies, Biotechnology, Botany, Business Administration, Computer Application, Chemistry, Economics, English, Odia, Political Science, Remote Sensing & GIS, Santali, Wildlife & Biodiversity Conservation, and Zoology

Vision & Mission


Vision

To provide access to quality higher education, shape thoughtful, creative, and well-rounded individuals, inculcate intellectual inquisitiveness, scientific temper, ethical and constitutional values, innovativeness, the spirit of service, and capabilities across disciplines including the sciences, the social sciences, the humanities, and professional, vocational and technical studies for improving quality of life, ensuring holistic development, and collective social wellbeing.

Mission

To become a self-reliant institution of eminence by pursuing academic and professional excellence. To make research activities and outreach initiatives more visible and accessible to indigenous tribal communities. To reduce inequalities and increase gender parity, ensure diversity, and inclusivity. To promote environmental awareness, collaboration, creativity, innovation and community engagement. To increase the enrolment, retention and graduation rates of students from economically, locationally marginalised and underrepresented sections. of society, and provide them with adequate institutional support. To lay emphasis on experiential learning of students and faculty to contribute richly to their academic attainment. To establish a transparent system for holistic and inclusive growth and development of the university, and ensure meaningful engagement of all stakeholders with the institution.

Our Journey


1998 (July 13): The university was officially established by the Government of Odisha under the Odisha Universities Act, 1989. It was created by carving out jurisdiction from Utkal Universit
1999: The university became fully operational at its permanent 98.84-acre site in Takatpur, Baripada.
2020 (dec23): To commemorate the 150th birth anniversary of the visionary philosopher-king of the princely state of Mayurbhanj, the state government officially changed its name from North Orissa University (NOU) to Maharaja Sriram Chandra Bhanja Deo University (MSCBU).
2023: Maharaja Sriram Chandra Bhanja Deo University (MSCBU) was officially awarded its premium 'A' Grade .
